About the shop

Run by Nesar Ahmed, for the neighbourhood

Star Stores is a general store on Samsul Huda Road in Park Circus, stocking the things a household reaches for most — rice, spices and cooking oil, fresh fruit and vegetables, dairy, bread, snacks and cleaning supplies. The idea is a simple one: keep the shelves full of what people actually need, and keep the shutter up late enough that a late errand isn’t a problem.

If something isn’t on the shelf, a phone call is the fastest way to ask.
